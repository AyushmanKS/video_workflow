# Research report: C0010 (scriptwriter)

**Topic:** the Ganga's birth. On the Ganga bank, at Rama's request, Vishvamitra begins the Ganga's origin (Bala Kanda 1.35.13–24):
- Himavan, lord of mountains, and his queen Mena, Meru's daughter, have two daughters: the Ganga, the elder, and Uma.
- The gods ask Himavan for the Ganga. By dharma, for the good of the three worlds, he gives her, and the gods take her to heaven.
- Uma takes a stern vow of austerity, and Himavan gives her to Rudra.
- The sage closes the account: this is how the river of three paths *first* went to the sky, rising to the world of the gods.

**Continuity:** Episode 09 ended on the teaser VR 1.35.12 (Rama asks how the Ganga of three paths crossed three worlds to the sea) with the card "Next: Ganga's Birth". Scene 1 pays this off.

## Variant readings and notes
- **1.35.20:** sa.wikisource reads उग्रं सुव्रतमास्थाय and valmikiramayan.net reads उग्रं सा व्रतमास्थाय, with the same sense ('took a stern vow'). Likewise कन्याऽसीद् रघुनन्दन / कन्यासीद्रघुनंदन. Neither is quoted.
- **Orthography only:** गङ्गा / गंगा and स्वच्छन्द / स्वच्छंद (1.35.14–18), and एतत् ते / एतत्ते (1.35.23). The verse card follows sa.wikisource.
- **Verse card split.** The card splits 1.35.18 at the pāda as हिमवांस् / तनयां. This keeps the sandhi हिमवांस्तनयां letter for letter. gu and te transliterate the card (Devanagari +0x180 / +0x300) and keep । and ॥.
- **1.35.19 'went'.** The verse says अगच्छन्, 'went'. valmikiramayan.net glosses 'came back to heaven'. The scripts take 'heaven' from 1.35.23–24 (खं गता; सुरलोकं समारूढा), not from the gloss.
- **'Sisters' and 'younger'.** 'Sisters' (सगी / सख्ख्या / సొంత in hi/mr/te) rests on 1.35.15, तयोर्माता (Mena is the mother of both), and on 1.35.16 and 1.35.22. 'Younger' renders द्वितीया, 'the second daughter'.
- **'Gave in marriage'.** This renders ददौ… सुताम्… रुद्राय (1.35.21), the idiom for giving a daughter in marriage; valmikiramayan.net glosses it the same way. (1.36.5, कृतोद्वाहः, confirms the marriage but is not used.)
- **Not narrated (length).** These are not narrated for length but are kept in the sources:
  - देवकार्यचिकीर्षया (1.35.17);
  - धातूनामाकरो (1.35.14), which appears in the visual only;
  - Mena's epithets सुमध्यमा and मनोज्ञा (1.35.15), rendered only as grace in the bible text;
  - लोकनमस्कृताम् (1.35.21);
  - 'best of rivers' (1.35.22) in en and te.
- **The three paths.** The scripts do not name the other two paths. The sarga does not list them, and valmikiramayan.net's note on 1.35.23 gives competing explanations, which are not used.
- **Himavan's feelings.** The scripts add no grief or reluctance for Himavan; the text says only धर्मेण, 'by dharma'.

## Later traditions
None are used.
- Uma is not called Parvati in the narration.
- Sati's rebirth, Kama, the Puranic austerity episodes, Bhagiratha and the Ganga in Rudra's locks are all out of scope.
- No regional retelling was consulted.
