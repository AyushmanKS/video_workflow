## Interpretation (judgement, labelled)
- **All three topics are evergreen** rather than spiking; the upward en Wikipedia movement is modest and season-agnostic.
- **B has the strongest Shorts format fit:** 76% of top English results are Shorts. Its autocomplete shows a specific, under-served question ("16 qualities"). It is also the exact payoff promised at the end of Episode 01.
- **A is a strong next episode (Episode 03),** but Hindi results are dominated by long devotional kathas from big creators (saturated at the top). It has a natural seasonal peak at Ram Navami (Mar/Apr).
- **C's huge view counts come from TV-serial uploads.** Low recent-upload performance plus missing Wikipedia data mean weaker evidence, and it is out of chronological order for the series.
- **Caution:** recent-upload medians are low for every topic (most new uploads get few views). Success depends on hook and retention, not topic choice alone.
