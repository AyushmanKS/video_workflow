## Corrections to the brief
- **Mithila speech:** spoken by the sages, with Vishvamitra at their head (1.31.5).
- **Day, not night:** the sixth day, not a night (1.30.7).
- **Not twins:** "two sons" (1.18.14).
- **Payasa:** comes at the putriya ishti (1.15.2–3, 1.16.11).

## Later traditions
- **Venkatesha Suprabhatam:** opens with VR 1.23.2 and was composed 1420–1432 CE (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suprabhatam, accessed 2026-11-18). The narration labels it as later tradition and names no place.
- Nothing else from later retellings is used.
