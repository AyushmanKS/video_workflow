## Narration

1. Kill them, said the sage. Rama spared one.
2. At Siddhashrama, Vishvamitra began his sacrifice, sworn to silence.
3. For six days and nights, the princes guarded it without sleep.
4. On the sixth day, Rama told Lakshmana: be ready. The altar fire blazed up.
5. A roar filled the sky. Like monsoon clouds, the rakshasas swept down.
6. Maricha and Subahu rained blood upon the altar.
7. Rama loosed the Manava weapon. It flung Maricha a hundred yojanas into the ocean.
8. It stuns him, Rama told Lakshmana, but does not take his life.
9. Then Agneya, the fire weapon, felled Subahu. The wind weapon took the rest.
10. The sacrifice was complete. Siddhashrama had earned its name, said the sage.
11. At dawn, the sages spoke of a bow in Mithila no god or rakshasa could string.
