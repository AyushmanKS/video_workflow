# Strategy: C0007 (scriptwriter)

**Decision:** continue narrative order and pay off the Ep06 teaser (1.29.23, "here you must kill them") in the very first line, then reverse it. Format: 35–55 s, hard limit under 60 s; all five lint estimates are 52.5–53.6 s.

- **Angle:** the order was to kill, and Rama spares one. He uses the Manava weapon on Maricha, which stuns him and hurls him a hundred yojanas into the ocean without taking his life (1.30.17–20), then kills Subahu with the Agneya and the rest with the Vayavya (1.30.22–23). No motive is added; the text's own words are the reveal.
- **Hook (selected in every language), contrast / request with a twist:**
  - en: "Kill them, said the sage. Rama spared one." (2.66 s)
  - hi: "वध का आदेश था। श्रीराम ने एक को छोड़ा।" (2.78 s)
  - gu: "વધનો આદેશ. રામે એકને બક્ષ્યો." (2.58 s)
  - te: "చంపమన్నా రాముడు ఒకణ్ణి వదిలాడు." (2.70 s)
  - mr: "वधाचा आदेश. श्रीरामांनी एकाला सोडले." (2.82 s)
  - Every hook is under the 3.0 s hard limit and over the 2.2 s lint advisory. The en hook is about 2.5–2.7 s on purpose, since the 2.0 s Ep06 en hook failed the Kokoro speaker check.
  - Frame 0 reads muted: Rama from behind, a shadow rakshasa hurled away toward the ocean.
- **Beats (11 scenes):**
  - H1/H2: scene 1 (1.29.23 payoff and reversal).
  - C: scenes 2–3. Siddhashrama; the sage's vow and silence (1.29.30, 1.30.4); six days and nights sleepless (1.30.5).
  - E: scenes 4–7. Day six, "be ready", the fire blazes (1.30.7–8); the roar and monsoon-cloud rakshasas (1.30.10–11); Maricha and Subahu rain blood (1.30.12); the Manava hurls Maricha into the ocean (1.30.17–18).
  - T: scene 8, verse card 1.30.20: "it stuns him but does not take his life". The main loop closes here, at about 65–67%.
  - P: scenes 9–10. Agneya fells Subahu, Vayavya the rest (1.30.22–23); the sacrifice completes and Siddhashrama "earns its name" (1.30.25–26). Ends by 84–88%.
  - N: scene 11.
- **Open loops:**
  - Scene 1 (who was spared, and how?) is answered in scene 7 and closed by the verse card in scene 8.
  - Scene 5 (what are they doing?) is answered in scene 6.
  - Scene 6 (what will Rama do?) is answered in scenes 7–9.
  - Scene 11 opens Ep08.
- **Silences:** 0.5 s after scene 7 (before the Turn), 0.6 s after the verse card (before the Payoff), 0.4 s after scene 10 (before Next).
- **Loop type:** composition. Scene 11 reuses frame 0's framing (Rama from behind), with the hurled rakshasa replaced by the road to Mithila and a glowing bow on the horizon.
- **Teaser (one line), VR 1.31.9 (vulgate):** "At dawn, the sages spoke of a bow in Mithila no god or rakshasa could string." The setting comes from 1.31.6–7. The Next card reads "Next: Janaka's Bow" (1.31.11) and is ≤3 words in every language.
- **Engagement line:** none in the video, because gu/te/mr have no seconds to spare. Pinned-comment share prompt: "Which weapon spared Maricha?" The answer in the text is Manava (1.30.17–20).
- **Depiction (direction for the creative director, who writes the prompts):**
  - Rama is always seen from behind.
  - The rakshasas are human-shaped shadow silhouettes with exactly two arms and wild hair, with no wings, horns or gargoyles (character bible, Tataka and Ep04 notes).
  - The blood is dark red rain on the altar, with no gore.
  - The Manava, Agneya and Vayavya are radiant, fiery and wind-swirl arrows.
  - Use the series tokens {rama_teen}, {lakshmana_teen} and {vishvamitra}.
- **Languages:** en (Kokoro af_heart), and hi, gu, te, mr (Maya 2 Malini). gu, te and mr are in the `unreviewed-language` cohort and have no native review.
