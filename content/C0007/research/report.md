## Reference corrections and notes
- **Sarga 29 numbering on sa.wikisource is still broken** (most verses carry ॥१-२९-२५॥), as in Ep06. Locators 1.29.23 and 1.29.26–32 come from valmikiramayan.net, where the verse locators read "१-२९-२३", "१-२९-३०" and so on.
- **1.30.22–23 split differs.** sa.wikisource ends 1.30.22 at "…आग्नेयं रघुनन्दनः" and puts Subahu's fall plus the Vayavya in a three-line 1.30.23. valmikiramayan.net puts Subahu's fall in a three-line 1.30.22 and the Vayavya in 1.30.23. Both agree on the range, so the scripts cite **1.30.22–23**.
- **1.30.15–16 split differs.** valmikiramayan.net prints 15 + 16a and 16b + 17; sa.wikisource prints 15 and 16 as full couplets. The numbers agree.
- **Readings on the verse card 1.30.20:** sa.wikisource वियुज्यते, valmikiramayan.net व्ययुज्यत. The card follows sa.wikisource (as Ep06 did); the meaning is the same.
- **Other readings, not quoted:** 1.30.5 अनिद्रं (sa.wikisource) / अनिद्रौ (valmikiramayan.net); 1.30.18 समाहतः / समाहितः; 1.30.24 विजये / विजयी.
- **Who speaks 1.30.4:** the verse follows the sages' praise (1.30.3), and valmikiramayan.net attributes it to "the other sages". No script names the speaker; the scripts narrate only its content (the vow of silence, the six-day watch).
- **"Spared":** the text never gives a motive. It records Rama's words "I am not eager to kill such ones" (1.30.16) and "it stuns him but does not take his life" (1.30.20). The valmikiramayan.net note that Maricha is kept alive for the golden-deer episode is **commentary**, not Valmiki, and is not used. 1.30.22–24 kill all the others, so "spared one" is exact.
- **"Monsoon clouds":** 1.30.11 compares the rakshasas covering the sky to a cloud in the rainy season (प्रावृषि). Hindi काली घटा, Gujarati ચોમાસાનાં વાદળ, Telugu వానమబ్బులు and Marathi पावसाळी ढग keep the simile.
- **Stringing the bow:** आरोपणम् (1.31.9) is stringing the bow, not merely lifting it. Hindi चढ़ाना, Gujarati ચડાવવું, Telugu ఎక్కుపెట్టు and Marathi प्रत्यंचा लावणे all mean stringing.
- **Lakshmana** is present throughout (1.30.5–7, 1.30.14–20). He is named in en, hi, gu and mr; in te he is implied by అన్నదమ్ములు (brothers) for length.
- **Not narrated:** Rama's anger as he looses the Manava (परमक्रुद्धः, 1.30.17) and the sages honouring him like Indra (1.30.24). Omitted for length; no calm or angry framing is added either way.
- **Depiction:** the rakshasas follow the character bible (`profiles/characters/ramayana.json`, Tataka entry and Ep04 note): human-shaped shadow silhouettes with exactly two arms and wild hair, no wings, horns or gargoyle forms. The rain of blood is shown off-body (dark red rain on the altar), per the sensitivity rule.
