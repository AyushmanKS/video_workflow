# Audience brief: C0004 (audience-localizer)

- **EN:** explain "rakshasa" once (demon), and "yajna" (sacred fire ritual) only if needed. The emotional core is a father's fear versus a king's word, which is universal.
- **HI:** viewers know Vishvamitra and the Tataka story from TV/Ramcharitmanas. Use Valmiki details as the surprise: ten nights only, the fainting king, "not yet sixteen", Vasishtha's counsel, and the Bala/Atibala mantras. Respect forms: महर्षि विश्वामित्र, गुरु वसिष्ठ, श्रीराम.
- **Depiction:** rakshasas as dark shadows in the sky over the altar, no gore. Ravana is mentioned only (symbolic shadow rule). Rama and Lakshmana appear as teenage princes, Rama seen from behind.
