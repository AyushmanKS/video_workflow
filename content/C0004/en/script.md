## Scenes

1. A king promised a sage anything he wanted. Then the sage asked for his son.
2. One day the great sage Vishvamitra arrived at the court of Ayodhya.
3. Overjoyed, King Dasharatha welcomed him and promised to grant whatever he wished.
4. Two demons, Maricha and Subahu, were defiling his sacred fire ritual. He needed Rama to protect it, for ten nights.
5. Hearing this, the king fainted for a moment. Then he spoke.
6. My lotus-eyed Rama is not yet sixteen. I do not see him fit to fight demons.  
   _on screen:_ ऊनषोडशवर्षो मे / रामो राजीवलोचनः। / न युद्धयोग्यतामस्य / पश्यामि सह राक्षसैः॥ (Valmiki Ramayana  1.20.2)
7. Take my army instead, he begged. Take me.
8. And when he heard that the demons were sent by Ravana, his fear only grew.
9. Vishvamitra rose in fury. A king of the Raghu line, breaking his word?
10. Then the royal guru Vasishtha spoke. Keep your promise, he said. With Vishvamitra, Rama is safe.
11. So the king let Rama go. And Lakshmana, his loyal brother, walked beside him.
12. On the banks of the Sarayu, Vishvamitra taught them two mantras, Bala and Atibala, so they would never tire or hunger.
13. But their first test waited in a dark forest. A demoness with the strength of a thousand elephants.
