## Narration

1. Vishvamitra's own sister became a river.
2. That night by the Shona, he told Rama of his elder sister, Satyavati.
3. True to her husband Richika, she followed him to heaven in her own body.
4. Then she flowed forth as the Kaushiki, a holy river for the good of the world.
5. So he made his home beside her on the Himalayan slopes, out of love.
6. Only his vow drew him away, a vow, he said, that Rama's splendour had fulfilled.
7. Past midnight, he told Rama to sleep, so nothing would hinder their road.
8. Around them, trees stood still, birds lay hidden, and the cool moon rose.
9. And Rama, a little wonderstruck, slept beside Lakshmana.
10. At dawn they set out, and by midday reached the Ganga, alive with swans.
11. There, Rama asked how this river crossed three worlds to reach the sea.
