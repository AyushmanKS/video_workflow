# Audience brief: C0003 (audience-localizer)

## English
- Many viewers know Rama's birth only vaguely. The surprising specifics are the hook: a sacred pudding shared in exact fractions, and twins for Sumitra.
- Explain "Ashvamedha" once and "payasa" (sweet rice pudding) once.

## Hindi
- The birth story is very familiar from Ramcharitmanas and TV ("भये प्रगट कृपाला"). Make clear the episode follows Valmiki's account; avoid Tulsidas phrasing.
- The surprise is Valmiki's exact detail: the sharing of the khir (आधा, चौथाई, आठवाँ) and the four portions of Vishnu.
- **Register:** warm storytelling; use खीर/पायस naturally; श्रीराम, राजा दशरथ, महारानी कौशल्या.

## Sensitivity
- Depict Vishnu and the newborn respectfully: Vishnu as symbolic radiant light; the infant glowing and seen with the mother from behind or side.
- Ravana appears as a menacing silhouette, not gore.
