## Narration

1. देव एका अजन्म्या बाळाला घाबरले.
2. रामाच्या तीन मार्गांच्या प्रश्नावर ऋषींनी उमेची कथा छेडली.
3. रुद्राचा उमेशी विवाह झाला, आणि दिव्य शंभर वर्षे सोबत राहिले.
4. पुत्र झाला नाही, तरी देव घाबरले की येणाऱ्या बाळाला कोण सोसेल.
5. देवांनी विनवले, लोकहितासाठी रुद्रांनी तेज आतच धरावे.
6. रुद्र तयार झाले, पण विचारले की जागलेले तेज कोण धरेल.
7. देवांनी पृथ्वी निवडली, आणि तेज तिच्या डोंगर-वनांवर पसरले.
8. अग्नी व वायू त्यात शिरताच, ते श्वेत पर्वत व बोरूवन बनले.
9. इच्छित पुत्रापासून वंचित उमेने रागाने देवांना शापले, की त्यांच्या पत्नी निःसंतान राहतील.
10. पृथ्वीलाही शापले, की तिला पुत्रसुख लाभणार नाही.
11. रुद्र उमेसह हिमवानावर तपाला गेले. ऋषी म्हणाले, आता गंगेची कथा ऐका.

## Verse card (scene 5)

> त्रैलोक्यहितकामार्थं / तेजस्तेजसि धारय। / रक्ष सर्वानिमाँल्लोकान् / नालोकं कर्तुमर्हसि॥
> वाल्मीकि रामायण 1.36.11 (दाक्षिणात्य पाठ)

Meaning (1.36.11): "For the good of the three worlds, hold your power within your power; protect all these worlds, do not unmake them." It is the gods' plea to Rudra, and it echoes Ep10's card (त्रैलोक्यहितकाम्यया, 1.35.18). The narration gives the meaning while the card is on screen; the verse is not recited. Four pādas of eight syllables; sa.wikisource reads सर्वानिमाँल्लोकान् and valmikiramayan.net सर्वानिमान् लोकान्न…, the same words with a different sandhi. Script: Devanagari.

## Accuracy notes

- Every narrated fact maps to a verse in `research/report.md`. No dialogue is invented: all speech (the gods' plea, Rudra's question, the gods' answer, the curses, the sage's last line) is reported, and `quote_src` is set on scenes 2, 5, 6, 7 and 11.
- 'The sage began with Uma' rests on 1.36.4–5 (the sage tells the whole tale) and 1.36.27, where he names what he has told as the mountain daughter's account (शैलपुत्र्या… विस्तरः). Rama and Lakshmana both ask (1.36.1–4); every language names Rama (review F8), and the visual shows both.
- The union (1.36.5–6) is narrated only as 'wed, and dwelt as one for a hundred years of the gods' (कृतोद्वाहः; दिव्यं वर्षशतं गतम्), per the sensitivity plan. Nothing physical is narrated or drawn.
- 'The gods wondered who could withstand the child to come' (review F4; hi keeps ऐसे बालक) renders 1.36.8 (यदिहोत्पद्यते भूतं कस्तत् प्रतिसहिष्यति). 'Led by Brahma' (पितामहपुरोगमाः, 1.36.7) appears in the visual (Brahma's lotus leads the orbs) and in the source table, not in the narration, for length.
- 'Power' renders तेजः throughout (radiance, energy). The commentary's anatomical gloss is not used anywhere, and nothing anatomical is drawn.
- 'Hold his power within, for the good of the three worlds' renders the verse card (त्रैलोक्यहितकामार्थं तेजस्तेजसि धारय, 1.36.11) with 1.36.10 (न लोका धारयिष्यन्ति तव तेजः).
- 'Who would bear the power already stirred' renders 1.36.14 (यदिदं क्षुभितं स्थानान्… धारयिष्यति कः). 'The gods chose the Earth' condenses 1.36.15 (तद्धरा धारयिष्यति); 'spread over hills and woods' renders सगिरिकानना (1.36.16).
- 'Agni entered it with the Wind' renders 1.36.17 (आविश त्वं… वायुसमन्वितः); 'a white mountain and a reed forest' renders श्वेतपर्वतम्… दिव्यं शरवणम् (1.36.18). The glow 'like fire and the sun' (पावकादित्यसंनिभम्) is in the visual only.
- Kartikeya's birth in the reed forest (1.36.19) and the gods' worship of Uma and Shiva (1.36.19–20) are not narrated: the birth is told in sarga 37, and the brief keeps it for the next episode.
- Uma's curse on the gods (1.36.20–22) is told as the text gives it (review F1): she was stopped when she longed for a son (निवारिता… पुत्रकाम्यया), so she curses as one denied the son she longed for, in anger (समन्युः), that their wives would bear no children (अप्रजाः सन्तु पत्नयः). Her reddened eyes (क्रोधसंरक्तलोचना) are not drawn; she is drawn grave and dignified.
