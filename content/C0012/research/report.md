# Research report: C0012 (scriptwriter)

**Topic:** Bala Kanda sarga 36, Uma's story. On the Ganga bank, Rama and Lakshmana ask how the Ganga came to flow on three paths (1.36.1–4). Vishvamitra answers with the mountain daughter's account first (1.36.5–27):
- Rudra marries Uma, and a hundred divine years pass with no son.
- The gods, led by Brahma, fear the being that would be born. They beg Rudra to hold his power (तेजः) within, for the three worlds.
- Rudra agrees and asks who will bear what has stirred. The Earth receives it, and Agni, joined with Vayu, enters it. It becomes the white mountain and the divine reed forest.
- Uma, stopped when she longed for a son, curses the gods to have no children by their own wives, and curses the Earth.
- Rudra goes with Uma to a peak of Himavat for tapas. The sage then turns to the Ganga's origin (1.36.27).

**Continuity:** Ep10 ended on 1.35.23–24 with the card "Next: Three Paths", and scene 2 pays it off (1.36.1–4).

**Edition:** vulgate (southern recension).
- **Text:** sa.wikisource, रामायणम्/बालकाण्डम्/सर्गः ३६. Raw wikitext fetched 2026-11-23.

- **Sarga 37:** read only to label the Next card.

## Variant readings and notes
- **1.36.2:** ज्येष्ठाया / ज्येष्ठाय.
- **1.36.3:** प्लावयेल् / पावयेल्, and कथं गङ्गां / गङ्गा.
- **1.36.5:** पुरा राम कृतोद्वाहः / पुरा रामकृतोद्वाहः (word division).
- **1.36.11 (verse card):** सर्वानिमाँल्लोकान् नालोकं / सर्वानिमान् लोकान्नालोकं. Same words, different sandhi; the card follows sa.wikisource. 4 pādas of 8 syllables. gu and te transliterate the card and keep the candrabindu, । and ॥.
- **1.36.13:** तेजसैव / तेजस्येव.
- **1.36.19–20:** सुप्रीतमनसस्तदा / ततः.
- **1.36.24:** सुदुर्मेधो / सुदुर्मेधे.
- **1.36.25:** पीडितान् (with व्रीडितान् noted as a variant).
- **1.36.27:** सहलक्ष्मण / सहलक्ष्मणः.
- **तेजः** is rendered as "power" (तेज, తేజస్సు). valmikiramayan.net's anatomical gloss and its tantric note are not used.
- **"Dwelt as one"** renders 1.36.5–6 under the owner's sensitivity wording; the verb of 1.36.5b is not rendered.
- **"The sage began with Uma"** rests on 1.36.27, where the sage names what he has told as शैलपुत्र्या विस्तरः.
- **Direction:** west (1.36.25), then the northern side of a peak arising from Himavat (1.36.26). Narrated only as "to Himavan".
- **Not narrated:** पितामहपुरोगमाः (visual only), Rudra's epithets, the worship in 1.36.19–20, क्रोधसंरक्तलोचना, बहुभार्या and सुदुर्मेधे.

## Later traditions
None are used. Uma is not called Parvati. Kama, Sati, Tarakasura, the Krittikas and the six-faced child are out of scope.
