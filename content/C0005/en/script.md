## Scenes

1. Rama's first enemy had the strength of a thousand elephants. And he did not want to kill her.
2. Vishvamitra led the two young princes across the Ganga. In mid-river, the waters roared. · title card: **Across the Ganga**
3. On the far bank lay a dreadful forest. Once, the sage said, this was the rich land of Malada and Karusha.
4. Then a yakshi named Tataka laid it all to waste. Even now, no one dared to pass. · title card: **Tataka**
5. Rama was puzzled. How could a yakshi hold such strength?
6. It was a boon, the sage said, from Brahma himself. · title card: **Brahma's Boon**
7. But when she and her son Maricha attacked the sage Agastya, his curse turned her into a man-eating rakshasi. · title card: **Agastya's Curse**
8. To protect the people, cruel or not, sinful or not, a protector must act. That is a ruler's eternal duty.
   > नृशंसमनृशंसं वा / प्रजारक्षणकारणात्। / पातकं वा सदोषं वा / कर्तव्यं रक्षता सदा॥ (Valmiki Ramayana  1.25.18)
9. So Rama twanged his bowstring. The forest shook, and Tataka came charging.
10. Still, he told Lakshmana, I cannot kill her. She is a woman. I will only stop her.
11. She raised a storm of dust, rained down rocks, then vanished into illusion. · title card: **Illusion**
12. Enough pity, cried the sage. Dusk is near, when demons grow stronger. Rama aimed by sound alone, and one arrow brought her down.
13. The gods rejoiced, and the cursed forest bloomed again. Next morning, the sage had a gift for Rama: divine weapons. · title card: **Next: Divine Weapons**
