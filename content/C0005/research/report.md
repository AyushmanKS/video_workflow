**Corrections to the handoff outline:**
- Brahma's boon is the source of her strength (1.25.7). She was cursed after attacking Agastya with Maricha, following Sunda's death.
- Rama's hesitation appears in two places: his own words during the fight (1.26.12) and Vishvamitra's prior argument (1.25.17–19). Rama does not refuse the mission (1.26.4).
