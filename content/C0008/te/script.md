## Narration rewrite (2026-11-17)

The owner rejected the first render: the sentences read like clipped headlines and broke between scenes. The narration is rewritten as one continuous telling, the way a kathavachak tells it aloud: every scene is a complete thought that grows out of the one before (linked with so/then/but/yet or the language's own connectives), speech is reported or given a soft lead-in instead of a bare quote after a colon or dash, scene 3 no longer ends on a bare question, and no sentence is split across a scene break.

Unchanged: the 11 scenes and their beats, every image field (`visual`, `seed`, `shot`, `angle`, `move`, `transition`, `accent`, `overlay`), the verse card, the hook, the title, the teaser, and the facts in `research/report.md` (nothing added beyond the verse table). Changed: `narration`, `tts`, `keywords`, and the planned silences (0.5/0.5/0.3 s instead of 0.5/0.6/0.4 s to make room for the longer, linked sentences).

## Accuracy notes

- Every narrated fact maps to a verse in `research/report.md`. No invented dialogue: spoken lines are reported speech condensed from 1.31.23, 1.32.16–17, 1.32.18–22, 1.33.6–9 and 1.34.5–8 (`quote_src` set on those scenes).
- Scene 2 pays off the Ep07 teaser (1.31.9) with 1.31.10: mighty kings tested the bow and could not string it. The departure north is 1.31.16; the princes travel with the sage (1.31.21).
- The story is told at the Shona camp the same night (1.31.19–24; 'half the night has passed', 1.34.14). The script does not say the daughters' city was 'here': the Shona land is Vasu's (1.32.8–10) and Kushanabha's city is Mahodaya (1.32.6).
- The daughters' refusal (scenes 6–7) keeps the text's two reasons: they could unseat Vayu but guard their tapas (1.32.20), and only their father gives them in marriage (1.32.21–22). No motive is added for Vayu beyond desire and rage (1.32.16, 1.32.23).
- 'Forgiveness' is the father's word for what they did (क्षान्तं क्षमावतां, 1.33.6). The script does not say the daughters chose to forgive in their own words.
- The name Kanyakubja (Kannauj) does not appear in the vulgate text of sargas 32–33 on either site and is not used.
- 'Grandfather' joins two adjacent verses (1.34.5 Gadhi is Kushanabha's son; 1.34.6 'that Gadhi is my father'); no later tradition is used.
- Not narrated: Kusha's four sons and their cities (1.32.1–10), the gandharvi Somada and Chuli's mind-born son (1.33.11–18), Kusha's blessing for Gadhi (1.34.2–4). Omitted for length.
- The CE and Goldman were not checked, so there are no `[CE]` tags. sa.wikisource prints 1.31.19 twice; the 1.31.19–23 locators follow valmikiramayan.net.
- Hook కన్యలు renders कन्याशतम्; రాకుమార్తెలు was 3.5 s and failed the hook limit.
- Scene 2 names Lakshmana (రామలక్ష్మణులతో); the sage takes honorific plural (నడిచారు, చెప్పారు, అన్నారు).
