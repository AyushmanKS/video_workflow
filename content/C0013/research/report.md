# Research report: C0013 (scriptwriter)

**Topic:** Bala Kanda sarga 37, the birth of Kartikeya, as Vishvamitra tells it on the Ganga bank (1.37.1–32).
- While Rudra does tapas, the gods with Indra, led by Agni, go to Brahma seeking a commander (1.37.1–4).
- Brahma: Uma's word stands, the gods will have no offspring by their own wives; but through Agni and the sky-Ganga the gods' commander will come, and the Ganga will honour him, to Uma's joy (1.37.5–8).
- On Kailasa, adorned with metals, the gods charge Agni; he releases the radiance (तेजः) into the Ganga, and all her streams fill (1.37.9–14).
- The Ganga cannot hold the risen radiance; at Agni's word she sets it down on Himavat's side (1.37.15–18).
- From it arise gold and silver, copper and iron, and from its dross tin and lead; the forest on the mountain turns golden, and gold is called जातरूप from then on (1.37.18–22).
- The gods engage the Krittikas to nurture the newborn; they resolve "he is the son of us all"; the gods name him Kartikeya, and Skanda; he becomes six-faced (1.37.23–28).
- In one day (एकाह्ना) the tender child conquers the daitya hosts, and the gods, led by Agni, anoint him commander of the gods' armies (1.37.29–30).
- The sage closes the Ganga's account and the birth of Kumara, with a blessing on Kartikeya's devotees (1.37.31–32).

**Continuity:** Ep11 ended on 1.36.27 with the card "Next: The Gods' Commander". Scene 1 (hook) and scene 2 pay it off: the gods seek a commander (1.37.1), and the hook names the answer, a newborn child (1.37.23–30).

## Variant readings and notes
- **1.37.2:** सुराराम (sa.wikisource) / सुराः राम (valmikiramayan.net). Same words; the wikisource form runs them together.
- **1.37.7:** इयमाकाशगङ्गा च (sa.wikisource) / इयमाकाशगंगा (valmikiramayan.net, without च).
- **1.37.13:** समन्तादवशीर्यत / समंतादवकीर्यत. Either way "spread all about"; not narrated.
- **1.37.16–17:** valmikiramayan.net notes पार्श्वे with the variant पादे ("at the foot of Himavat"). The narration says "Himavan's slope", true to both.
- **1.37.18–19:** हिरण्यम् is glossed "silver" by valmikiramayan.net (paired with काञ्चनम्, gold). The narration says "gold and silver", following that gloss.
- **1.37.23:** सह मरुद्गणाः / सहमरुद्गणाः (word division).
- **1.37.27:** गर्भपरिस्रवे / गर्भपरिस्रवात्. Not narrated.
- **1.37.29:** एकाह्ना ("in one day") can be read with गृहीत्वा क्षीरम् (having taken the milk for one day) or with अजयत् (conquered in one day). valmikiramayan.net reads it with the milk and adds "became an adolescent", which is commentary. After story review F2 the narration does not use एकाह्ना: scene 10 says "Still tender, the six-faced child routed the demons" (सुकुमारवपुः, which is unambiguous; hi कोमल बालक, gu કોમળ બાળ, te పసివాడైన, mr कोवळ्या). The owner can restore "in one day" at G1 as an accepted reading (the instrumental can govern अजयत्). The hook avoids the point ("a newborn child", resting on जातमात्रस्य 1.37.24 and सुकुमारवपुः 1.37.29).
- **1.37.32:** पुत्रपौत्रैश्च / पुत्र पौत्रश्च. Not narrated.
- **Colophon:** sa.wikisource closes सप्तत्रिंशः सर्गः ॥१-३६॥, a typo for १-३७ on the source page.
- **तेजः** is rendered "radiance" (hi/mr तेज, gu તેજ, te తేజస్సు). "Rudra's radiance" in scene 4 follows the context of 1.36.17 (Agni entered Rudra's power) and 1.37.11 (तेज उत्सृज); 1.37.15 calls it Agni's own risen radiance (तेजस्तव), so the card's meaning is given as "such fire".
- **गर्भ** (1.37.12, 17, 21, 26–27) and the nursing in 1.37.28 are not rendered in any language, per the sensitivity plan. valmikiramayan.net's anatomical glosses on 1.37.12–28 are not used.
- **Commander:** सेनापति (1.37.1, 3, 7) and सुरसेनागणपति (1.37.30). en says "anointed him commander" (अभ्यषिञ्चन्); hi/gu/te/mr say "made him commander", for length.
- **Kailasa** (1.37.10) is where the gods charge Agni; the handing over itself happens at the Ganga (1.37.12). Scene 4 says "On Kailasa, the gods bade Agni…", which keeps the place with the charge.

## What is not narrated
- 1.37.8–9 (the Ganga will honour the son, to Uma's joy; the gods worship Brahma): length.
- 1.37.13 (the Ganga takes a divine form; Agni spreads about her): sensitivity plan 1.
- The गर्भ wording throughout, the meaning of स्कन्द (1.37.26–27) and the nursing half of 1.37.28: sensitivity plan 1, 4 and 6.
- जातरूप as the name of gold (1.37.22): length; offered as an optional pinned-comment fact.
- The Maruts and Indra (1.37.1, 23): visual only (the orbs), for length.
- 1.37.32 (phalashruti): outside the story.

## Later traditions
None are used:
- No Tarakasura: the text says only दैत्यसैन्यगणान्, "the daitya hosts".
- No Kama, Sati or Parvati: Uma is Uma.
- No spear, peacock or Murugan/Subrahmanya iconography.
- Nothing about the six Krittikas beyond 1.37.23–28: no six babies joined into one, no Saravana-pond bathing story from the Puranas.
