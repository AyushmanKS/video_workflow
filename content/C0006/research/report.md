## Reference corrections and notes
- **Sarga 29 numbering on sa.wikisource is broken.** Most verses carry ॥१-२९-२५॥, so it cannot be used for locators. The teaser verse number **1.29.23** was taken from valmikiramayan.net, where its verse locator reads "१-२९-२३". The Siddhashrama naming is at 1.29.3–4 by sequential count; valmikiramayan.net also groups 1.29.3–4.
- **Sarga 28 on sa.wikisource numbers two verses "१-२८-२१".** The second one should be 22. That affects only 1.28.21–22, which are not used.
- **Variant in the on-screen verse 1.28.2:** sa.wikisource reads संहारान् (plural), valmikiramayan.net reads संहारं (singular). The card follows sa.wikisource; the meaning is the same.
- **Variant at 1.27.1:** मधुरस्वरम् on sa.wikisource, मधुराक्षरम् on valmikiramayan.net. The verse is not quoted.
- **"Recall" / "withdrawal":** saṃhāra here is the technique for withdrawing a released weapon, not "destruction" (its common modern Hindi sense). The Hindi narration says लौटाना rather than संहार to avoid that misreading, and Telugu uses ఉపసంహార only in the on-screen title.
- **No motive is added.** The text does not say why Rama asked for the withdrawals, and no script supplies a reason.
- **Count of weapons:** more than forty names appear in 1.27.4–20 by the scriptwriter's count. The scripts make no count claim; "dozens" appears only in an unselected hook.
- **Lakshmana** is not mentioned in 1.27–28, so he is not narrated; he appears only as a figure in scene 2's visual direction.
