## Narration

1. Rama's new weapons bowed before him.
2. The morning after Tataka, Vishvamitra smiled. Take all my weapons, he told Rama.
3. The discus of Dharma. The discus of Time. The Vajra thunderbolt.
4. The Brahma weapon. Weapons of fire, of wind, even of sleep.
5. Facing east, the sage gave him their mantras, rare even for gods.
6. As he chanted, the weapons stood before Rama, palms joined. We serve you, they said.
7. Rama touched them and said: dwell in my mind.
8. Now not even gods can overcome me, Rama said. But teach me how to call them back.
9. So the sage taught him the recall. Those powers bowed too, like embers, smoke, sun and moon.
10. Go now, Rama told them. Come to my mind when I need you.
11. Ahead lay Siddhashrama. Here, said the sage, the rakshasas come. Here you must strike them.
