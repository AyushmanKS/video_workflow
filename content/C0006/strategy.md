# Strategy: C0006 (scriptwriter)

**Decision:** continue narrative order and pay off the Ep05 teaser ("Next morning, the sage had a gift for Rama: divine weapons", 1.27.1–2) in scene 2. This is the series' first episode in the 35–55 s format, with a hard limit under 60 s.

- **Angle:** the gift is not the climax. The weapons arrive alive, bow and offer service (1.27.24–26), and Rama, now "unassailable even by the gods", asks for one more thing: how to call them back (1.28.2). Power, then the power to recall it. The scripts add no motive the text lacks; the contrast is left to the viewer.
- **Hook (selected in every language):** "Rama's new weapons bowed to him." / श्रीराम के आगे झुके अस्त्र। / અસ્ત્રો રામ સામે ઝૂકી ગયાં. / అస్త్రాలే రాముడికి మొక్కాయి. / अस्त्रे श्रीरामांपुढे झुकली.
  - Type: in medias res / forbidden detail.
  - Spoken 1.3–2.2 s by the lint rates.
  - Frame 0 reads without sound (the embodied weapons bowing).
- **Beats (11 scenes, 45–55 s):**
  - H1/H2: scene 1.
  - C: scenes 2–3, the Ep05 teaser payoff and the gift.
  - E: scenes 4–7, from the named weapons to the mantras, the weapons appearing and "dwell in my mind".
  - T: scene 8, the verse card 1.28.2: the request to recall.
  - P: scenes 9–10. The withdrawal powers bow like embers, smoke, sun and moon; "go, come to my mind when I need you". The main loop closes at about 80%.
  - N: scene 11.
- **Open loops:**
  - Scene 1 (why do weapons bow?) is answered in scene 6 and closed in scene 9.
  - Scene 8 (can they be called back?) is closed in scenes 9–10.
  - Scene 11 opens Ep07.
- **Silences:** 0.5 s after scene 7 (before the Turn), 0.6 s after the verse card (before the Payoff), 0.4 s after scene 10 (before Next).
- **Loop type:** composition. Scene 11 reuses frame 0's framing: Rama from behind, with the light figures replaced by the path to Siddhashrama.
- **Teaser (one line):** "Ahead lay Siddhashrama. Here, said the sage, the rakshasas come. Here you must strike them." This is **VR 1.29.23 (vulgate)**. The Next card reads "Next: Maricha, Subahu" (they attack at 1.30.12; both were introduced in Ep04 at 1.19.5–6).
- **Engagement line:** none in the video. Pinned-comment suggestion: "Which of these weapons does Rama use on Maricha?" The answer is Manava (1.27.16 → 1.30.17), revealed in Ep07.
- **Depiction:** the weapons in bodily form are radiant figures of light, not deities and not armoured warriors. Brahma's weapon is symbolic light. Vishvamitra and Rama use the series character tokens. Image prompts belong to the creative director.
- **Languages:** en (Kokoro af_heart), and hi, gu, te, mr (Maya 2 Malini). Marathi is new to the series. gu, te and mr have no native review.
