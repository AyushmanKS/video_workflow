# Instagram Niche Competitors: Indian Mythology / Ramayana / Mahabharata Storytelling

Research date: 2026-11-16. Method: free web search plus fetching press and analytics pages. Instagram profile pages could not be fetched directly, so bios and profile pictures are rarely visible in sources. Each item says whether it is verified and where it comes from. Follower counts are copied only where a source shows them, with that source's date. None are estimated.

## Which accounts do mythology storytelling, and what do their handles, names, bios and profile pictures look like?

### Takeaway
I found 19 real accounts. They fall into four types:
- **Face-led creators:** @thetemplegirl, @fictionwithdivyansh / @mahabharatwithdivyansh, @zoreverr.
- **Faceless keyword pages:** @mythverse.official, @hinduism, @hinduism_telugu_, @mythologyforyou.
- **Brand or studio accounts:** @amarchitrakatha, Epified, @sacredstudio.in.
- **Art or visual accounts:** @xstonedape, Punam Art Academy.

Most display names are either a person's name or a plain keyword ("Hinduism", "MythVerse", "Hinduism Telugu"). Bio text and profile-picture type were almost never visible in free sources, so most of those fields are gaps.

### Inferences
- There are two naming styles that work:
  - **Persona or creator plus topic:** "The Temple Girl", "Mahabharat with Divyansh", "Stories with Vishal".
  - **Pure keyword brand:** "MythVerse", "Hinduism Telugu".
- The largest individual accounts in this sample are face-led (Temple Girl, Divyansh, Zorawar). Faceless keyword pages reach roughly 50K–600K in HypeAuditor's 2026 list.
- A dedicated series handle helps searchability. Divyansh split @mahabharatwithdivyansh out from his personal @fictionwithdivyansh.
- Regional reach is commonly handled with a separate handle per language (@hinduism_telugu_, @hinduismmalayalam, @ramayanam.tamil), not one account in many languages.
- For a faceless channel posting in four languages, this suggests two options:
  - Put a keyword in the display name (for example "Mr. AKS Tales | Ramayana in Chalk"). Instagram search matches the name field, and every keyword page above relies on this.
  - Later, consider language-specific handles if one language takes off.

### Gaps
- Exact bio text, emoji use, link-in-bio tools and profile-picture type (logo, deity art or face) could not be confirmed for almost any account, because Instagram blocks fetches and press articles don't describe them.
  - Fill these by manually checking the profiles in a browser.
- No Gujarati mythology storytelling account was found.
- No Telugu Ramayanam Instagram creator was found beyond the general @hinduism_telugu_.
- No Sanskrit or shloka page was found within the search budget.
- No English-language diaspora explainer account was surfaced by search.
- Current follower counts are not available for @amarchitrakatha, Epified's Instagram, @xstonedape or @storieswithvishal.

## What differentiates the most-followed accounts?

### Takeaway
The accounts with the strongest evidence of large audiences have three things in common:
- a recognisable persona or narrator voice;
- a named, episodic series format with one-minute Reels and cliffhangers;
- a visual signature (temple footage, cinematic VFX, dark-academia grading).

Accounts that are only a keyword sit lower in the HypeAuditor sample.

### Cited Findings
- Temple Girl: 2.2M followers, a curated archive of about 400 posts, a persona name, and her own rules about respectful filming. — [AltBollywood](https://www.altbollywood.com/post/namratha-mohan-the-temple-girl-instagram-amazon-kids-app)
- Mahabharat with Divyansh: one-minute serial narration of a single epic, reaching 100K followers in 11 days. — [t2ONLINE](https://t2online.in/goodlife/books/divyansh-mundra-on-his-life-being-shaped-by-stories-and-the-internet-and-his-book-mahabharat-2025/1447132)
- Zorawar Singh: a named series ("Tattletales of Trinity"), a strong visual in the first 3 seconds, and episodes that end on cliffhangers. — [AltBollywood](https://www.altbollywood.com/post/zoreverr-zorawar-singh-content-strategy-mythology)
- The top Hinduism-category pages are mostly generic keyword names, from 50K to 616K followers (2026). — [HypeAuditor](https://hypeauditor.com/instagram-category-hinduism/)

### Inferences
- A one-epic, serial structure (Divyansh) is the closest match to @mr.aks.tale's plan of telling the Valmiki Ramayana episode by episode. That account's growth is the strongest evidence that serialised epic retelling works on Reels.
- Naming the series ("Ramayana in Chalk" or similar) as distinct from the handle follows the Zorawar and Divyansh pattern.

### Gaps
- There is no reliable, dated, cross-account follower comparison for creator-led mythology pages. Engagement rates are behind HypeAuditor's paywall and were not used.

## Is chalk-art / chalkboard mythology unoccupied on Instagram?

### Takeaway
I found no Instagram account dedicated to chalk or chalkboard retellings of the Ramayana or other mythology. The nearest examples are:
- a single viral chalk drawing of Hanuman (Punam Art Academy);
- whiteboard-animation mythology (Epified, mainly on YouTube);
- sand art of Ramayana episodes.

The niche looks open, but that is based on absence of evidence in web search only.

### Cited Findings
- The chalk Hanuman drawing on a blackboard by Punam Art Academy went viral on Instagram. It was a one-off art demonstration, not a story series. — [India TV News](https://www.indiatvnews.com/trending/offbeat/desi-woman-draws-lord-hanuman-using-chalk-with-both-hands-without-looking-viral-video-2023-07-02-878912)
- Epified makes whiteboard-animated mythology. — [The Better India](https://thebetterindia.com/70280/vijayendra-mohanty-interview-epified-channel)
- A sand artist recreated Ramayana episodes such as Bharat Milap in Ayodhya. — [DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/amp/story/ayodhya-sand-artist-recreates-episodes-of-ramayana)
- A Mandya drawing teacher sketched the whole Ramayana on one page. The medium is not stated. — [Udayavani](https://www.udayavani.com/english-news/creativity-at-its-best-mandya-drawing-teacher-explains-ramayana-in-one-sketch)
- Searches for "chalk art Ramayana Instagram" and for "Ramayana in chalk" / "Epic in chalk" found no matching account. The results were dominated by Kalamkari, Pattachitra and Cheriyal sellers. — [Memeraki example](https://www.memeraki.com/collections/ramayana/products/ramayana-kalamkari-painting-by-harinath-n-1)

### Inferences
- "Chalk" is a distinctive word that nobody in this niche seems to own. Using it in the display name ("…in Chalk") would differentiate the account.
- Epified's whiteboard look shows that board-drawn mythology storytelling has an audience.

### Gaps
- Instagram hashtag search (#chalkart #ramayan #chalkboardart) was not possible without logging in. Small chalk-mythology accounts may exist that web search doesn't index. Do a manual in-app check before finalising.

## Sensitivities: deity depiction, devotional versus neutral tone, and backlash cases

### Takeaway
There is a pattern of public outrage, police complaints and recalls over **AI-generated or distorted images of deities** in 2024–2026. A new channel should avoid AI-rendered deity faces and anything that reads as altering sacred iconography. Successful accounts range from devotional to purely entertaining. Accuracy to scripture is now publicly checked even for viral hits.

### Cited Findings
- **Puri Jagannath (Odisha):** the temple administration filed a police complaint over AI videos of Lord Jagannath, and 97 AI-generated "misleading" images of the temple deities were removed. — [Odisha Bytes](https://odishabytes.com/97-ai-generated-misleading-images-of-puri-jagannath-temple-deities-removed-after-outrage/); [Organiser, 21 Jan 2026](https://organiser.org/2026/01/21/336054/bharat/odisha-ai-generated-videos-of-bhagwan-jagannath-spark-outrage-puri-temple-administration-files-police-complaint/amp/); [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fodisha%2Ffake-ai-generated-videos-of-lord-jagannath-surface-police-complaint-filed-3867751)
- **MAMEE (Malaysia):** the company recalled snack bags after AI artwork of Lord Murugan drew backlash. The deity was reportedly shown with the wrong weapon (a trident instead of the vel). — [Marketing-Interactive](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/mamee-recalls-bags-after-ai-artwork-of-lord-murugan-sparks-backlash); [ContentGrip](https://www.contentgrip.com/mamee-ai-packaging-recall/)
- **Ganesha "Ghibli-style" AI images:** a Mumbai Ganesh mandal called for AI pictures of Bappa to be removed, saying it is not appropriate to transform his image this way. — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/mumbai/mumbai-news-ghibli-art-of-lord-ganesha-an-insult-mumbai-cha-raja-mandal-calls-for-removal-of-ai-pictures-of-bappa-from-social-media)
- **"God in Car" AI trend:** a police complaint was filed over an AI video showing deities in an SUV. — [LatestLY](https://www.latestly.com/social-viral/rinku-singh-in-trouble-over-god-in-car-viral-ai-trend-video-7283608.html)
- **Ekta Kapoor's show:** she was criticised for an AI-made Lord Krishna scene in 'Kyunki'. — [Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/?p=3501323)
- **South Africa:** an artist in Phoenix came under fire for AI images featuring Hindu deities (Oct 2024). — [Business Report](https://businessreport.co.za/thepost/news/2024-10-06-phoenix-artist-under-fire-for-ai-images-with-hindu-deities)
- **AI video with Jesus:** an FIR was filed over an AI video showing Shiva and Vishnu calling Jesus their father (June 2026). — [Organiser](https://organiser.org/2026/06/28/360088/bharat/attack-on-hindu-faith-using-ai-video-claims-jesus-is-the-father-of-hindu-deities-hindu-groups-protest-fir-filed/)
- **Accuracy scrutiny:** a viral @xstonedape reel was checked point by point against Puranic descriptions. — [AltBollywood](https://www.altbollywood.com/post/shiva-nandi-kailash-viral-reel-puranic-accuracy-xstonedape)
- **Disputes over versions:** coverage of a November 2026 blockbuster Ramayana film reports Hindu concern that the "core meaning is lost". It notes that versions differ; for example, the Lakshman-rekha motif is not in Valmiki. — [Religion News Service, 12 Nov 2026](https://religionnews.com/2026/11/12/in-a-blockbuster-retelling-of-the-ramayana-some-hindus-worry-the-core-meaning-is-lost/)
- **Respectful practice:** Temple Girl films only what temples allow and visits as a devotee first. — [AltBollywood](https://www.altbollywood.com/post/namratha-mohan-the-temple-girl-instagram-amazon-kids-app)
- **Range of tone:** at one end, Divyansh's tone is "fun, fast, modern" and his comments are mostly friendly; at the other, Zorawar's series reimagines deities as superheroes. Both appear to be well received. — [t2ONLINE](https://t2online.in/goodlife/books/divyansh-mundra-on-his-life-being-shaped-by-stories-and-the-internet-and-his-book-mahabharat-2025/1447132); [AltBollywood](https://www.altbollywood.com/post/zoreverr-zorawar-singh-content-strategy-mythology)
- **Low-effort AI devotional reels:** mass-produced, ready-made "AI Hindu gods reels" bundles are sold for devotional pages. This points to a crowded, low-differentiation segment of AI devotional content. — [superprofile.bio listing](https://superprofile.bio/vp/800aihindugodsreel)

### Inferences
- Hand-drawn chalk is a safer visual identity than photoreal AI deities. Most backlash targets AI images that distort or trivialise iconography.
  - Keep iconography correct (weapons, attributes).
  - Avoid mash-ups and "trend" treatments of deities.
- Saying in the bio that the source is the Valmiki Ramayana (for example "Valmiki Ramayana, told in chalk") guards against "wrong version" criticism, given that versions are publicly disputed.
- A neutral-respectful tone (storytelling, not preaching) is consistent with the larger creator accounts. Occasional devotional sign-offs are common in the category but not required.

### Gaps
- No source was found that systematically surveys whether successful pages avoid showing deities' faces.
- No source was found on how often top pages use "Jai Shri Ram"-style language in bios. Check this manually on the profiles.

A free web search for "Ramayana in chalk", "Epic in chalk" and "Mr AKS" returned **no matching account, brand or artist**. I found no well-known collision. This search was not a trademark search.

### Cited Findings

### Gaps

- Instagram handle availability for @ramayanainchalk and similar names was not checked. Check in the app.
