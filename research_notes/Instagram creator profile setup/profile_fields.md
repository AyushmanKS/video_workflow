# Instagram Creator profile fields and settings for discoverability (as of Nov 2026)

Context: new faceless storytelling account @mr.aks.tale, series "RAMAYANA — The Epic in Chalk" (chalkboard-art Reels, EN/HI/GU/TE), Indian and diaspora audience. Zero cost. Meta Verified is paid, so it is excluded.

Source labels: **[OFFICIAL]** = Instagram, Meta or Adam Mosseri. **[SECONDARY]** = industry blogs or news. **[OLD]** = published before 2024, so possibly outdated.

Method note: the help.instagram.com articles render through JavaScript and came back empty when fetched (502981923235522 and 1631821640426723 both returned only "Help Center"). Help Center facts below therefore come from search snippets of official pages, or from about.instagram.com and creators.instagram.com pages that did fetch.

## Display Name field (limit, search indexing, change frequency, keywords)

### Takeaway
Instagram's official search explainer says search matches your query against usernames, bios, captions, hashtags and places, and it advises using a handle or profile name related to your content. The display name is limited to about 30 characters. The rule that it can be changed only twice in 14 days is widely reported by third parties but was not confirmed from an official page in this research.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL][OLD, Aug 25 2021, Mosseri] "The text you enter in the search bar is by far the most important signal for Search." Instagram tries to match that text with "relevant usernames, bios, captions, hashtags and places." The second signal is your activity (accounts you follow, posts you viewed, past interactions). The third is popularity (clicks, likes, shares and follows for an account, hashtag or place) — [Instagram: Breaking Down How Instagram Search Works](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/break-down-how-instagram-search-works/)
- [OFFICIAL][OLD 2021] Tip: "Using an Instagram handle or profile name that's related to the content of your posts" is your best bet. If people know you by a particular name, include it in your username or profile name — [same source](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/break-down-how-instagram-search-works/)
- [OFFICIAL][OLD, Aug 2022, updated Apr 2023] Creators blog: "Use a handle or profile name related to your content" — [Instagram for Creators: Recommendations eligibility tips](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators)
- [OFFICIAL][OLD, May 31 2023] Search has its own ranking, "tailored to how people use it." The post publishes no Search signal list beyond this — [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- [SECONDARY] The name field allows "around 30 characters" and is separate from the username — [replug.io: Instagram bio character limit](https://replug.io/blog/instagram-bio-character-limit)
- [SECONDARY] The display name can be changed twice within 14 days — [Vista Social](https://vistasocial.com/insights/how-to-change-name-on-instagram/); [Beebom](https://beebom.com/how-change-instagram-name-and-username/amp/); [SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/blog/how-to-change-your-name-on-instagram/). This conflicts with [HandleGrab](https://www.handlegrab.com/blog/instagram-username-rules) and [Ecwid](https://www.ecwid.com/blog/how-to-change-instagram-name.html), which apply the 14-day rule to the username. No official text was retrieved.
- [SECONDARY, Feb 2025] Semrush repeats Mosseri's three signals and recommends keywords in the profile name — [Semrush: Instagram SEO](https://www.semrush.com/blog/instagram-seo/). Other blogs suggest a "Name | niche keyword" format and note that the name field is easier to change than the handle — [Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/instagram-seo/); [Later](https://later.com/blog/instagram-seo/amp/)

### Inferences
- The name field is an official, low-cost place for a keyword. A name such as "Ramayana in Chalk | Mr AKS" (about 26 characters) puts "Ramayana" in a searchable field while keeping the brand. Count characters in the app, because the em dash and spaces count.
- Because of the probable 2-per-14-days limit, finalize the name before launch rather than A/B testing it.
- Native-script keywords (रामायण, રામાયણ, రామాయణం) cannot all fit in 30 characters. Put them in the bio or captions instead (see the Bio section).

### Gaps
- No official 2024–2026 Instagram statement was found that confirms or updates the 2021 search-field list, or that says explicitly whether the display name, as distinct from the username, is matched. The 2021 tip mentions the "profile name", which implies it is.
- The exact display-name limit and change frequency could not be fetched from help.instagram.com.
- No controlled study was found that measures how much name keywords add to search reach.

## Username rules and the cost of changing it later

### Takeaway
Usernames allow up to 30 characters: letters, numbers, periods and underscores, case-insensitive and shown in lowercase. @mr.aks.tale (11 characters) is valid. After a change, the old handle is reportedly held for 14 days so you can revert. Changing breaks old @mentions and links, so pick the handle once.

### Cited Findings
- [SECONDARY] The limit is 30 characters, with only letters, numbers, periods and underscores allowed — [HandleGrab: username rules](https://www.handlegrab.com/blog/instagram-username-rules); [SlashGear 2023](https://www.slashgear.com/1192072/how-to-change-your-username-on-instagram-in-2023)
- [SECONDARY] The old username is held for 14 days, so you can switch back within that window if nobody else claims it — [Beebom](https://beebom.com/how-change-instagram-name-and-username/amp/); [SlashGear](https://www.slashgear.com/1192072/how-to-change-your-username-on-instagram-in-2023)
- [SECONDARY] There is no official limit on username changes, but frequent changes may trigger a review — [socialz.ai](https://socialz.ai/blog/how-to-change-name-on-instagram). This is contested; see the 14-day conflict in the Display Name section.
- [OFFICIAL][OLD 2021] The username is one of the fields matched by search text — [Instagram Search explainer](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/break-down-how-instagram-search-works/)

### Inferences
- "tale" in the handle is a weak genre keyword. "ramayana" is not in the handle, which is fine for a multi-series brand as long as the name and bio carry it.
- Periods in handles are easy to mistype in speech or voiceover ("mr dot aks dot tale"). Consider also securing mr_aks_tale or mraks.tale as defensive variants (free) if available.
- Old @mentions do not redirect after a change (common reporting, not verified), which is another reason to treat the handle as permanent.

### Gaps
- No official statement was found on whether a handle change affects ranking or recommendation eligibility. No evidence was found that it does.

## Bio: 150 characters, keywords, links, emojis, scripts, line breaks, CTAs

### Takeaway
Bio text is limited to 150 characters, counting spaces, emojis and line breaks. Instagram officially lists bios among the fields matched by search and tells creators to include keywords describing who they are and what the profile covers. Up to 5 links, each with an optional title, are supported, but only the first shows directly on the profile.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL][OLD 2021] "Include keywords about who you are and what your profile is about in your bio." Location-specific accounts can add their location — [Instagram Search explainer](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/break-down-how-instagram-search-works/)
- [OFFICIAL][OLD 2022/2023] Make sure your "About you" section includes keywords that explain who you are and what your profile covers — [Instagram for Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators)
- [OFFICIAL][OLD 2022/2023] Profile elements that break Recommendation Guidelines, "such as your profile photo or profile information", can make the whole account ineligible for recommendation — [Instagram for Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators)
- [SECONDARY] The limit is 150 characters including spaces, punctuation, line breaks and emojis, and "has held steady for years" — [replug.io](https://replug.io/blog/instagram-bio-character-limit)
- [SECONDARY][OLD, Apr 2023 launch] Up to 5 links via Edit profile > Links > Add external link, each with an optional title such as a CTA, reorderable by dragging. Only the first link shows on the profile and the rest sit behind a pop-up. Available to personal and professional accounts — [TechCrunch Apr 18 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/04/18/instagram-takes-on-linktree-and-others-with-support-for-up-to-5-links-in-bio/); [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/instagram-now-allows-up-to-5-links-in-bio-395742); [SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/blog/how-to-add-a-link-to-instagram-bio)
- [SECONDARY] Professional accounts get category labels and contact buttons outside the 150-character bio, which saves bio space — [replug.io](https://replug.io/blog/instagram-bio-character-limit)
- [OFFICIAL][OLD 2021] Comments are not used to find posts. Put keywords and hashtags in captions — [Instagram Search explainer](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/break-down-how-instagram-search-works/)

### Inferences
- A suggested structure within 150 characters: line 1 states the promise and keyword ("Valmiki Ramayana retold in chalk ✍️"), line 2 lists the languages ("EN • हिंदी • ગુજરાતી • తెలుగు"), line 3 gives the cadence and a CTA ("New episode daily ⬇️ Start at Ep 1"). Devanagari, Gujarati and Telugu characters take noticeable space, and some emoji count as 2 or more characters. Verify the count in the app.
- Including at least one native-script keyword (for example रामायण) may help match native-script queries, since matching is text-based. This is an inference from the official text-matching statement, not something Instagram has stated.
- Link 1 should be the highest-value free destination, such as the YouTube playlist, with a clear title like "Watch all episodes". Links 2–5 might be the per-language YouTube channels or playlists.

### Gaps
- No official confirmation was found of how non-Latin scripts and emoji count toward the 150 characters, or whether transliterated and native-script queries cross-match.
- No official statement says whether bio link titles are searchable.

## Profile picture: sizes, crop, formats, legibility, faceless branding

### Takeaway
No official Instagram spec was retrieved. Third-party consensus is a square upload of at least 320×320 (1080×1080 recommended for sharpness), shown as a circle of about 110 px on mobile. Keep the key mark inside the central circle.

### Cited Findings
- [SECONDARY] Upload 320×320 minimum, with 1080×1080 suggested. Mobile display is about 110×110, and desktop figures vary (150, 152 or 180 px). The circular mask trims the corners, by one estimate about 22% of the square — [Postfa.st profile specs](https://postfa.st/sizes/instagram/profile); [icons8](https://blog.icons8.com/articles/instagram-avatar-size/); [FirstPier](https://www.firstpier.com/resources/instagram-profile-picture-size); [Dash Hudson](https://dashhudson.com/blog/best-picture-format-instagram-dimensions)
- [OFFICIAL][OLD 2022/2023] A profile photo that violates Recommendation Guidelines can make the account ineligible for recommendations — [Instagram for Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators)

### Inferences
- For a faceless chalk brand, a single bold chalk mark (for example a bow-and-arrow or "AKS" monogram in white chalk on near-black slate) will read better at 110 px than a detailed character illustration. Fine chalk texture turns to noise at that size.
- Use high contrast (white or saffron on dark slate), keep the mark within the central ~70% diameter, avoid text smaller than about 1/4 of the height, and preview at 110 px before uploading. Use JPG or PNG (format support is not officially confirmed here).

### Gaps
- No official Instagram profile-photo spec page was retrieved. All dimensions are third-party and vary.
- No evidence was found comparing logo and character avatars for reach or follow rate.

## Creator account setup: categories, labels, contact, Facebook, dashboard, account status, privacy, other features

### Takeaway
The category is a descriptive label that can be shown or hidden. No official evidence was found that the category or the Creator vs Business type affects reach. What official sources do tie to discoverability: the account must be public (Creator and Business accounts are public automatically), it must pass Account Status recommendation eligibility, content must be original (no watermarks or reposts), and keywords must sit in the name, bio and captions.

### Cited Findings
**Categories and label**
- [SECONDARY, citing Help Center] Choose the category that best describes what you do. The category does not decide whether you get a Creator or Business account — [SuperProfile](https://superprofile.bio/guides/instagram-professional-account)
- [SECONDARY] Creator category examples include Digital Creator, Video Creator, Artist, Gamer, Writer and Blogger. The category label appears under the profile name and can be hidden — [ContentStudio](https://contentstudio.io/blog/instagram-creator-accounts); [predis.ai category list](https://predis.ai/resources/?p=83887); [G2](https://learn.g2.com/instagram-creator-accounts)
- [SECONDARY] Account type and category do not affect reach. Ranking depends on how people respond to content — [Kontentino](https://www.kontentino.com/q-and-a/brands-vs-creators-instagram-rules/); [Social Media Examiner](https://www.socialmediaexaminer.com/instagram-creator-accounts-what-marketers-need-to-know/); [SocialRails](https://socialrails.com/blog/instagram-business-vs-personal-account-guide). None quotes Mosseri directly.

**Public account and recommendation eligibility**
- [OFFICIAL] Only public accounts are eligible to be recommended. Professional accounts can check eligibility in Account Status, and eligibility does not guarantee recommendation — [Instagram Help Center snippet](https://help.instagram.com/653964212890722)
- [OFFICIAL][OLD 2022/2023] Creator and Business accounts are set to public automatically. Account Status (Settings > Account > Account status) shows whether content can be recommended to non-followers in Explore, Reels and Feed suggestions. Since April 2023 it also shows Search and Suggested Accounts eligibility. It lists violating content and profile elements, and violating content still reaches followers. Repeated violations make the whole account ineligible "for a period of time" — [Instagram for Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators); [Instagram Ranking Explained, May 2023](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- [OFFICIAL][OLD 2023] Recommendation Guidelines apply to Explore, Reels, Search, Suggested Posts and Suggested Accounts. Examples of content that is allowed but not recommended include depictions of violence — [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- [OFFICIAL][OLD 2022/2023] Originality: avoid reposts, watermarks and blurry or low-quality Reels. Use borderless 9:16 high-resolution video, hook viewers in the first 3 seconds, and use Insights to filter reach by followers vs non-followers — [Instagram for Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators)
- [OFFICIAL][OLD 2023] Reels that are low-resolution, watermarked, muted, bordered, mostly text, or already posted on Instagram are shown less — [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)

**Google and Bing indexing (new since mid-2025)**
- [SECONDARY] From July 10 2025, public posts from professional (Creator or Business) accounts run by users aged 18+ can appear in Google and Bing results. It is on by default and can be turned off in privacy settings. Coverage disagrees on how new this is — [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/instagram-content-becomes-searchable-on-google-starting-july-10/); [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-indexing-on-google/); [Search Engine World](https://www.searchengineworld.com/its-live-instagram-posts-are-ranking-and-showing-in-google-and-bing-seo-meets-social)

**Hashtags (affects captions, related to search)**
- [SECONDARY reporting an OFFICIAL Mosseri announcement, Dec 18 2025] Instagram capped hashtags at 5 per post and per Reel, down from 30 — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/no-more-hashtag-stuffing-instagram-limits-hashtag-usage-to-just-5-per-reel-and-post-507444-2025-12-19). Whether comment hashtags count toward the cap is unclear — [creatorflow.so](https://creatorflow.so/blog/how-many-hashtags-instagram/)

**Pinned posts**
- [SECONDARY][OLD, Jun 2022] Up to 3 posts or Reels can be pinned to the top of the profile grid via ⋯ > "Pin to your profile". Only grid posts can be pinned. Sources conflict on whether a 4th pin auto-unpins the oldest — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2331677); [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2022/06/07/you-can-now-pin-photos-to-your-instagram-profile-grid/); [Social Media Examiner](https://www.socialmediaexaminer.com/instagram-marketing-how-to-pin-posts-and-reels-to-a-profile/)

**Pronouns**
- [SECONDARY][OLD, May 2021] An optional pronouns field allows up to 4 pronouns from Instagram's suggestions, shown in grey beside the name. It does not use bio characters — [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/news/instagram-bio-gets-dedicated-pronoun-section); [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/instagram-pronouns-profiles)

**Security**
- [OFFICIAL] Authenticator-app 2FA can only be turned on in the Android or iPhone app, via Accounts Center > Password and security > Two-factor authentication. Up to 5 connected devices are allowed, and each needs its own authenticator app — [Instagram Help (iPhone)](https://help.instagram.com/iphone-app/566810106808145); [Instagram Help](https://help.instagram.com/iphone-app/1124604297705184)
- [SECONDARY, Oct 2025] 2FA setup walkthrough. Backup codes are under the same 2FA menu — [Malwarebytes](https://www.malwarebytes.com/fr/blog/how-to/2025/10/how-to-set-up-two-factor-authentication-2fa-on-your-instagram-account); [Storrito](https://storrito.com/help-center/how-to-review-your-instagram-security-settings-after-adding-2fa/)

**Paid (excluded)**
- Meta Verified is a paid subscription and is excluded under the zero-cost rule.

### Inferences
- Category choice: no evidence of a reach effect. Pick the label that best tells a 110-px-glancing viewer what the account is. "Video creator" or "Digital creator" fits Reels storytelling, "Writer" or "Artist" fit too, and "Education" may only exist as a Business category (unverified). The display-label toggle is cosmetic. Showing a clear label such as "Video creator" adds context at no cost.
- Contact info (email/phone) is optional. A dedicated brand email (free) shown via the email button keeps the owner's personal number private, in line with the project privacy rule. Turn "Display contact info" off if no inbox will be monitored.
- Facebook Page linking is optional. Nothing found ties it to Instagram reach. It is needed only for some cross-posting or API flows (the project already publishes via Instagram Login, per commit d1c130a/d1ba193 context).
- Keep the account public (the default for Creator accounts) and check Account Status after each early post. Chalk depictions of battles (for example Tataka in Ep05) are illustrated mythology, but "depictions of violence" is a listed non-recommendable category, so watch Account Status closely after violent episodes.
- Pin 3 Reels: Ep01 (series start), the best performer, and a trailer or "how to watch in your language" Reel.
- Turn on authenticator-app 2FA (not SMS), save backup codes offline, and review logged-in devices.
- Highlights: covers can reuse the chalk style with one label per language (EN, HI, GU, TE) or per arc (Bala Kanda, etc.). No official evidence ties highlights to reach.
- Close Friends is a Stories audience tool. Nothing found links it to non-follower discovery, so it can be ignored.
- If the owner is under 18, Google/Bing indexing does not apply (secondary sources).

### Gaps
- The official Help Center list of Creator categories, and whether "Education" or "Entertainment website" are offered to Creator (vs Business) accounts, could not be retrieved because the Help Center would not fetch.
- No official 2024–2026 statement was found on the "Display category label" or "Display contact info" toggles beyond secondary descriptions.
- No sources were found on the "Threads badge" (profile link to Threads) or on profile banners/channels as of 2026. Their effect on reach is unknown. No source covered the 2025 profile-grid change to a 3:4 (vertical) thumbnail crop in this research, so it is not asserted here.
- No reliable information was found on passkey support or an in-app "Security Checkup" for Instagram in 2026.
- No official statement was found on whether account age, a new account, or a Facebook link affects early distribution. Do not assume either way.
- The professional dashboard's specific contents were not researched in depth. The Insights followers/non-followers reach filter is officially documented (creators blog above).
