# Put "Ramayana in Chalk" where Instagram searches

Name the account **"Ramayana in Chalk | Mr AKS" (26 characters)**. Use a four-line bio of **141 characters** that has "Valmiki Ramayana", "chalk" and the word Ramayana in Devanagari, Gujarati and Telugu script. For the profile picture, use one bold white-chalk bow-and-arrow mark on dark slate, with no deity face. Instagram has said officially that search matches typed text against usernames, profile names, bios, captions, hashtags and places, and it tells creators to put keywords about what the profile covers into the name and bio. These two fields are the only free, official discovery levers on the profile itself. Everything else about reach depends on the Reels: watch time, likes per reach and **sends per reach** (which counts most for non-follower reach), a hook in the first three seconds, and staying **eligible for recommendation**. An account loses that eligibility if it posts watermarked or unoriginal video, breaks the Recommendation Guidelines (non-graphic violence is the one most likely to affect a Ramayana series), or fails to label realistic synthetic audio. Under zero cost, the setup work is mostly about avoiding self-inflicted penalties. Do that by keeping the account public, uploading the clean master render rather than a YouTube download, sending the AI self-disclosure flag, using no more than **5 hashtags** (the cap since December 2025), turning on authenticator-app 2FA and checking Account Status every week. The niche looks open: free web search found no Instagram account doing chalk retellings of mythology, and no account, brand or artist was found using "Ramayana in Chalk" or "Epic in Chalk". Throughout this report, **[OFFICIAL]** means Instagram, Meta or Adam Mosseri, either directly or as reported by press. **[INDUSTRY]** means third-party blogs and vendors. **[INFERENCE]** means reasoning from the evidence that no source states.

## Name, bio and picture: the three fields to get right on day one

### Display name: keyword plus persona, at most 30 characters

**[OFFICIAL]** Instagram's search explainer calls the typed query "by far the most important signal for Search". It says Instagram matches the query against "relevant usernames, bios, captions, hashtags and places", and it advises "using an Instagram handle or profile name that's related to the content of your posts" ([Instagram](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/break-down-how-instagram-search-works/)). The Creators blog repeats this advice ([Instagram for Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators)). Both pages date from 2021–2023. No newer official list of search fields was found, so the advice is old but has not been contradicted. **[INDUSTRY]** The name field holds about 30 characters ([replug.io](https://replug.io/blog/instagram-bio-character-limit)). Several sources say the name can be changed only twice in 14 days ([Vista Social](https://vistasocial.com/insights/how-to-change-name-on-instagram/)), though others attach that 14-day rule to the username instead ([HandleGrab](https://www.handlegrab.com/blog/instagram-username-rules)). Either way, finalise the name before launch and do not A/B test it.

**[INFERENCE]** The handle @mr.aks.tale (11 characters, valid) contains no topic keyword. The display name has to carry "Ramayana", and "Chalk" is the differentiator that nobody else in the niche uses. The pattern of persona plus keyword matches what the niche research found among successful mythology pages: "The Temple Girl", "Mahabharat with Divyansh", "MythVerse" and "Hinduism Telugu" ([HypeAuditor](https://hypeauditor.com/instagram-category-hinduism/); [t2ONLINE](https://t2online.in/goodlife/books/divyansh-mundra-on-his-life-being-shaped-by-stories-and-the-internet-and-his-book-mahabharat-2025/1447132)).

| Rank | Display name | Characters | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 (recommended)** | `Ramayana in Chalk \| Mr AKS` | **26** | Topic keyword first, so it survives truncation in search lists. The persona is kept for later series. |
| 2 | `RAMAYANA — The Epic in Chalk` | 28 | Exact series title, but it carries no persona and needs renaming when the series ends. |
| 3 | `Ramayana in Chalk · AKS Tales` | 29 | Close to the limit. "Tales" echoes the handle. |
| 4 | `Ramayana in Chalk \| रामायण` | 26 | Adds a native-script keyword but drops the brand. |
| Avoid | `Mr AKS Tales \| Ramayana in Chalk` | 32 | Over the limit. |

Counts are Unicode code points with spaces included. The em dash and middle dot each count as one character. Confirm the count in the app before saving.

### Bio: 141 characters, with Ramayana in three scripts

**[OFFICIAL]** Instagram says: "Include keywords about who you are and what your profile is about in your bio" ([Instagram](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/break-down-how-instagram-search-works/)). **[INDUSTRY]** The limit is 150 characters, and spaces, line breaks and emoji all count ([replug.io](https://replug.io/blog/instagram-bio-character-limit)). **[INFERENCE]** Matching is text-based, so writing रामायण, રામાયણ and రామాయణం in native script should let the bio match native-script searches that a Latin-only bio would miss. Instagram has not confirmed this, or how it counts non-Latin characters. Naming "Valmiki" also protects against "wrong version" complaints, which the niche research shows are common. A November 2026 film drew criticism partly because versions of the Ramayana differ ([Religion News Service](https://religionnews.com/2026/11/12/in-a-blockbuster-retelling-of-the-ramayana-some-hindus-worry-the-core-meaning-is-lost/)).

**Recommended bio (141 characters, 4 lines of 36 / 25 / 35 / 42):**

```
Valmiki Ramayana, retold in chalk ✍️
रामायण • રામાયણ • రామాయణం
English • Hindi • Gujarati • Telugu
AI-assisted art & voice · Start at Ep 1 ⬇️
```

**Fallback bio (133 characters, plain Latin text, also under 150 even if Instagram counted bytes):**

```
Valmiki Ramayana, retold in chalk, episode by episode
English • Hindi • Gujarati • Telugu
AI-assisted art & voice
Start at Ep 1 below
```

The ✍️ and ⬇️ emoji count as two code points each, and those are included in the 141. Paste the bio into the app and check the counter there. If it reads over 150, use the fallback. The "AI-assisted art & voice" line is **[INFERENCE]**. It costs little and covers you on two fronts. Meta requires disclosure of "realistic-sounding audio that was digitally created" and "may apply penalties" if creators don't disclose ([Meta Newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/02/labeling-ai-generated-images-on-facebook-instagram-and-threads/)). Instagram also reportedly restricts unlabelled AI-generated profiles **[INDUSTRY]** ([Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/instagram-renames-ai-creator-label-and-restricts-unlabeled-ai-generated-profiles/)). The trade-off is that India has had repeated backlash against AI images of deities (see the policy section). Being open about AI use up front is safer than having viewers discover it, but the line will draw some critical comments. Do not promise "new episode daily" in the bio, because the series runs one episode at a time, on request.

### Profile picture: a mark that reads at 110 pixels

**[INDUSTRY]** No official spec could be retrieved. Third-party consensus is to upload a square image of at least 320×320 (1080×1080 recommended). It shows as a circle of about 110 px on mobile, and the circular mask cuts off the corners ([Postfa.st](https://postfa.st/sizes/instagram/profile); [icons8](https://blog.icons8.com/articles/instagram-avatar-size/)). **[OFFICIAL]** A profile photo that breaks the Recommendation Guidelines can make the **whole account** ineligible for recommendation ([Instagram for Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators)).

**[INFERENCE] Brief:** Make a 1080×1080 PNG or JPG. The background is flat near-black slate (#1C1F1E or similar) with a faint chalk-dust texture at the edges only. The subject is one bold mark in white chalk: Rama's bow with a nocked arrow, drawn diagonally. One saffron accent (#F28C28) on the arrowhead is optional. Keep the whole mark inside the central circle, about 70% of the width, with thick strokes and no fine hatching, because chalk texture turns into noise at 110 px. Do not include text, letters or a deity's face. This avoids AI-deity sensitivities, avoids iconography mistakes, and stays legible. The mark should match the chalk style of the Reels so that the avatar works as the series' visual signature. Draw it by hand or with vector tools. Do not generate a deity portrait. Before uploading, preview it at 110 px on both a dark and a light phone background.

For each Reel, the pipeline has four API settings to decide. **[OFFICIAL]** `is_ai_generated` is "an optional parameter to provide a self-disclosure of AI usage in the post". The reference documentation does not show it in a Reels example, so test it on one dry-run container and confirm the "AI info" label appears ([Meta developer docs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/instagram-graph-api/reference/ig-user/media)). `audio_name` names the Reel's original audio and "you can only rename once". Set it to something like "Ramayana Ep05 Tataka · mr.aks.tale", so that anyone who reuses the sound links back to you (same source). `trial_params.graduation_strategy` (`MANUAL` or `SS_PERFORMANCE`) publishes a Trial Reel that is shown to non-followers first. It was added to the Content Publishing API in November 2025 ([Meta changelog](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/changelog)). **[INDUSTRY]** Eligibility needs somewhere between 200 and 1,000 followers, depending on the source ([Superprofile](https://superprofile.bio/guides/instagram-trial-reels); [CreatorFlow](https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-trial-reels/)). The pipeline should send it as optional and fall back to a normal Reel. Finally, the **hashtag cap is 5** ("If you include more than 5 tags… your comment won't post"). Mosseri says hashtags explain what a post is about but do not increase reach ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/no-more-hashtag-stuffing-instagram-limits-hashtag-usage-to-just-5-per-reel-and-post-507444-2025-12-19); [LatestLY](https://www.latestly.com/technology/instagram-caps-hashtags-at-five-per-post-in-new-policy-shift-amidst-head-of-instagram-mosseris-myth-claims-7245760.html)). **[INFERENCE]** A workable set is `#ramayana #ramayan #valmikiramayana` plus one language tag and one episode tag. Put the episode keyword in the first line of the caption, in both native script and transliteration (Tataka · ताड़का · તાડકા · తాటక).

## Four rules that can quietly cut reach to zero

**Originality and cross-posting from YouTube.** **[OFFICIAL]** Since April 2024, when Instagram finds near-identical content it recommends only the original. Recommended content must have "no visible watermark", and accounts that repeatedly repost others' content stop being recommended ([Instagram Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality)). Press reported a threshold of 10 or more unoriginal posts in 30 days ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/instagrams-algorithm-overhaul-will-reward-original-content-and-penalize-aggregators-130018977.html)). Mosseri has warned specifically against other platforms' watermarks ([Inman](https://www.inman.com/2025/03/07/head-of-instagram-reveals-secrets-to-social-media-success/)). In April 2026 the rule reportedly expanded to photos and carousels ([ALM Corp](https://almcorp.com/blog/instagram-restricts-reach-content-aggregators/)). **[INFERENCE]** No official text mentions cross-posting your own work from YouTube. The rules target content that isn't yours, so posting your own render after YouTube should be safe, provided Instagram receives the **clean master file**. Never upload a re-downloaded Short, and never include a YouTube logo or a "Subscribe" end card. Instagram has not confirmed this reading. It follows from the general rule.

**AI labelling.** **[OFFICIAL]** Disclosure is mandatory for "photorealistic video or realistic-sounding audio that was digitally created or altered" ([Meta Newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/02/labeling-ai-generated-images-on-facebook-instagram-and-threads/)). Labels show as "AI info", and they are applied either when Meta detects industry-standard indicators or when the creator discloses ([Meta Transparency Center](https://transparency.meta.com/governance/tracking-impact/labeling-ai-content)). In the app, the control is the "Label as made with AI" toggle ([Instagram Help](https://help.instagram.com/761121959519495)). **[INFERENCE]** Stylised chalk images are not photorealistic, but synthetic TTS narration arguably is realistic-sounding audio. Images from a local model carry no C2PA metadata, so Meta will not auto-label them, and self-disclosure is the only protection. **[INDUSTRY]** Blog claims that labelled content loses "15–80%" of its reach are unsupported, and Meta has announced no penalty for correctly labelled content ([Magic Hour](https://magichour.ai/blog/instagram-ai-generated-content-labels)).

**Recommendation Guidelines.** **[OFFICIAL]** Some content is allowed on Instagram but not recommended. The categories include violence, political or social topics, engagement bait, and low-quality or unoriginal content. While an account is ineligible, followers still see its posts but non-followers do not ([Instagram Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality)). No rule singles out religious or mythological content, and the exact wording on illustrated violence could not be retrieved. **[INFERENCE]** Keep scenes like the slaying of Tataka symbolic, with no blood and no lingering on injury. Keep modern politics and communal framing out of captions. Replace "Comment ॐ if…" or "Share to 5 people" with genuine questions.

**Deity sensitivity in India.** This is not an Instagram rule, but it decides whether the account survives in this niche. Between 2024 and 2026, AI-generated or altered deity images led to police complaints and removals. The Puri temple administration filed a complaint and 97 AI images of Jagannath were taken down ([Organiser](https://organiser.org/2026/01/21/336054/bharat/odisha-ai-generated-videos-of-bhagwan-jagannath-spark-outrage-puri-temple-administration-files-police-complaint/amp/); [Odisha Bytes](https://odishabytes.com/97-ai-generated-misleading-images-of-puri-jagannath-temple-deities-removed-after-outrage/)). A snack recall followed AI art of Lord Murugan holding the wrong weapon ([Marketing-Interactive](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/mamee-recalls-bags-after-ai-artwork-of-lord-murugan-sparks-backlash)), and a Mumbai mandal demanded removal of "Ghibli-style" AI images of Ganesha ([Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/mumbai/mumbai-news-ghibli-art-of-lord-ganesha-an-insult-mumbai-cha-raja-mandal-calls-for-removal-of-ai-pictures-of-bappa-from-social-media)). Even viral reels are now checked line by line against scripture ([AltBollywood](https://www.altbollywood.com/post/shiva-nandi-kailash-viral-reel-puranic-accuracy-xstonedape)). **[INFERENCE]** The chalk style is a natural buffer, because it is clearly an illustration and not a photoreal deity. Iconographic accuracy (weapons, attributes) still has to pass review at G2. This is also why the avatar avoids a deity face.

## What drives reach for a brand-new creator account in 2025–2026

**[OFFICIAL]** In January 2025 Mosseri named the three most important ranking signals: **watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach**. He said likes matter slightly more for reaching followers and sends matter slightly more for reaching non-followers ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/); [Kompozy](https://kompozy.io/news/instagram-mosseri-ranking-signals-guidance)). Since April 2024, every Reel is first tested on a small audience that includes non-followers and expands if it performs well ([Instagram Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality)). That is the structural reason a zero-follower account can break out. Mosseri calls the first three seconds "the hook" and says a Reel should have sound, whether a song, a voiceover or original audio ([Inman](https://www.inman.com/2025/03/07/head-of-instagram-reveals-secrets-to-social-media-success/)). Reels over three minutes are reportedly not recommended to new audiences ([Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers)). Your 60–90 second cuts are well inside that limit. Instagram has published no number for posting frequency. Mosseri reportedly advises whatever frequency you can sustain ([Bazzaal](https://www.bazzaal.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-mosseri)). The "official" claims you will see repeated, such as "sends are worth 3–5x likes", have no primary source behind them **[INDUSTRY]** ([SocialDay](https://socialday.live/features/instagrams-dm-share-signal-now-drives-3-to-5-times-more-reach-than-likes)). The same goes for India posting-time studies. These cluster around 6–9 PM IST, but some admit their figures are editorial baselines ([SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/insights/best-time-to-post-reels-on-instagram); [timetopost.co](https://timetopost.co/best-time-to-post/instagram/india/)). The existing YouTube slots (20:30 and 21:30 IST) fall in that window. Keep them as a hypothesis to test against Insights, not as established fact.

The niche evidence shows what has worked on top of those signals. The fastest-growing comparable page, "Mahabharat with Divyansh", told **one epic in one-minute serial episodes** and gained 100,000 followers in 11 days. That figure is the creator's own claim, reported by press ([t2ONLINE](https://t2online.in/goodlife/books/divyansh-mundra-on-his-life-being-shaped-by-stories-and-the-internet-and-his-book-mahabharat-2025/1447132)). @zoreverr's 830K audience is built on a named series, a strong visual in the first three seconds and cliffhanger endings ([AltBollywood](https://www.altbollywood.com/post/zoreverr-zorawar-singh-content-strategy-mythology)). Faceless keyword pages sit lower but are still substantial, between 50K and 616K in HypeAuditor's 2026 Hinduism list ([HypeAuditor](https://hypeauditor.com/instagram-category-hinduism/)). The steelman against a faceless account is real: the largest individual accounts in the sample are face-led. **[INFERENCE]** A faceless account has to make up for that with a consistent narrator voice, a recognisable visual signature (chalk, which nobody else uses) and serial cliffhangers that make people send episodes to family, because sends per reach is the signal that matters most for non-followers.

Running four languages on one account is the biggest open question. Regional mythology pages usually use **one handle per language** (@hinduism_telugu_, @hinduismmalayalam, @ramayanam.tamil) ([HypeAuditor](https://hypeauditor.com/instagram-category-hinduism/)). Instagram has documented no language targeting for organic Reels. **[INFERENCE]** Start with the single account as planned. Keep the language of each Reel's first caption line consistent with its audio, use the language Highlights to help people navigate, and read Insights by language after about 30 posts. If one language clearly takes off, especially Gujarati or Telugu where no competing Ramayana creator was found, spin it off to its own handle, following the Divyansh pattern of splitting out a series account.

## Conclusion

On Instagram, the profile is mostly a set of things to avoid getting wrong rather than a growth lever. The documented ways to lose reach (going private, watermarks, re-downloaded files, undisclosed synthetic voice, graphic violence, engagement bait) outnumber the documented ways to gain it (keywords in the name and bio). Most of the setup above is therefore about protection, and the work that drives growth happens in the first three seconds and the last line of each Reel. The account's real advantage is that it occupies a gap: free web search found no chalk-art mythology account, no Gujarati Ramayana storyteller and no Telugu Ramayanam creator, and the visual style itself steps around the AI-deity controversies that are hurting competitors. This rests on absence of evidence, so do a quick in-app search for #chalkart and #ramayan before launch.

Several points are still unverified. The help.instagram.com pages would not render, so the name-change limit, the exact Creator category list, how non-Latin characters count toward 150, whether `is_ai_generated` works on Reels, and the Trial Reels follower threshold all need a check in the app or a dry run. Search for the handle and confirm the "AI info" label on the first published Reel. Treat the first 30 posts as the experiment that turns this report's inferences into evidence from the account itself.
